## Prompt complet

Generate one complete French educational slide, exact 1672 × 941 px landscape raster. Institutional Opquast flat design: dominant white, generous whitespace, navy structure, restrained orange, light-blue and pale-green accents, rounded corners, little or no shadow, Arial or close sans-serif. No organic corner shapes.

Mandatory header: top left `QUALITÉ WEB` above `EXPÉRIENCES DURABLES`; top right `Opquast` above `DES SITES MEILLEURS POUR TOUS`.

Composition: one abstract proposal sheet at the center, with exactly three equal non-hierarchical outcome pictograms around it. Put the complete possible-verdicts sentence prominently above or beside the diagram. Place rule 87 in a distinct rule card, not as the automatic unique answer. Put the classroom warning in a strong but calm bottom callout.

Only these nine visible strings are allowed:

1. `QUALITÉ WEB`
2. `EXPÉRIENCES DURABLES`
3. `Opquast`
4. `DES SITES MEILLEURS POUR TOUS`
5. `Verdicts possibles : retravailler · recommander · rapprocher d'une règle existante`
6. `Règle 87 · Les processus complexes sont accompagnés de la liste de leurs étapes.`
7. `Ne jamais prétendre valider une règle officielle en classe.`
8. `RÈGLES OPQUAST · CONSTRUIRE UNE RÈGLE`

Do not impose one verdict, present the workshop as official, add a selected state, ranking, extra label, text, number, pseudo-text, logo, watermark, photo, 3D, gradient, breadcrumb or progress indicator. Generate one slide only.
