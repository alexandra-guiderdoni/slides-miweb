## Prompt complet

Generate one complete French educational slide as a raster image, exact 1672 × 941 px landscape. Institutional Opquast flat design: dominant white, navy structure, generous whitespace, rounded pastel cards of equal importance, little or no shadow, Arial or close sans-serif. No organic corner shapes.

Mandatory header: top left `QUALITÉ WEB` above `EXPÉRIENCES DURABLES`; top right `Opquast` above `DES SITES MEILLEURS POUR TOUS`.

Composition: a card `Proposition` enters a neutral central examination table labeled `Examen`. From it, four equal outcome cards fan out symmetrically, with no ranking, success/failure coding or preferred path. Use distinct simple neutral icons but equal size, weight and color intensity. The visual relationship means possible orientations after examination, not automatic causality.

Only these twelve visible strings are allowed:

1. `QUALITÉ WEB`
2. `EXPÉRIENCES DURABLES`
3. `Opquast`
4. `DES SITES MEILLEURS POUR TOUS`
5. `Proposition`
6. `Examen`
7. `Règle retenue`
8. `Recommandation`
9. `Non retenue`
10. `Évolution d'une fiche existante`
11. `RÈGLES OPQUAST · CONSTRUIRE UNE RÈGLE`
12. `04 / 16`

Do not imply that recommendation equals failure. Do not mark any outcome as good or bad. Do not add criteria, explanation, text, numbers, pseudo-text, logo, watermark, photo, 3D, large gradient, breadcrumb or progress indicator. Generate one slide only.

## Critères d'acceptation

- Douze chaînes exactes.
- Quatre sorties de même statut graphique.
- Aucune hiérarchie de valeur ni causalité ajoutée.
