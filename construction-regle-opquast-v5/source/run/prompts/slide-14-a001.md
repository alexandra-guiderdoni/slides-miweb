## Prompt complet

Generate one complete French educational slide, exact 1672 × 941 px landscape raster. Institutional Opquast flat design: dominant white, generous whitespace, navy structure, light-blue and pale-orange accents, rounded corners, little or no shadow, Arial or close sans-serif. No organic corner shapes.

Mandatory header: top left `QUALITÉ WEB` above `EXPÉRIENCES DURABLES`; top right `Opquast` above `DES SITES MEILLEURS POUR TOUS`.

Composition: a central case card contains the exact situation. Under it, four equal empty activity cards in a clean two-by-two grid contain only their numbered labels. The cards must visibly leave room for classroom work without lines resembling text. Place the pedagogical disclaimer below them. Do not provide any answer or correction.

Only these twelve visible strings are allowed:

1. `QUALITÉ WEB`
2. `EXPÉRIENCES DURABLES`

5. `CAS : « Je commence une longue démarche et j'ignore les étapes restantes. »`

Do not add rule 87, a correction, a prefilled answer, a duration, animation or facilitation instruction, extra label, text, number, pseudo-text, logo, watermark, photo, 3D, gradient, breadcrumb or progress indicator. Generate one slide only.
