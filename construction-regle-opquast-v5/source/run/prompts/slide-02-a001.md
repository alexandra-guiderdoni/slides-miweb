## Prompt complet

Use case: productivity-visual.

Generate one complete French educational slide as a raster image, including all text. Exact landscape canvas: 1672 × 941 px. Dominant white background, generous whitespace, centered navy title, institutional flat design, Arial or a close sans-serif, light blue information areas and pale orange vigilance area, rounded cards, little or no shadow. No organic corner shapes.

Mandatory header: top left `QUALITÉ WEB` above `EXPÉRIENCES DURABLES`; top right `Opquast` above `DES SITES MEILLEURS POUR TOUS`.

Composition: show one simple professional figure or neutral user icon on the left, clicking a clearly internal link represented without text. A simple horizontal path leads to a neutral missing-page symbol on the right and visibly stops. The relationship must mean: expected content is not reached and the journey is interrupted. Place the need in a light blue card and the prejudice in a pale orange card. Do not show data loss or certain abandonment.

Only these nine visible strings are allowed, reproduced character for character:

1. `QUALITÉ WEB`
2. `EXPÉRIENCES DURABLES`
3. `Opquast`
4. `DES SITES MEILLEURS POUR TOUS`
5. `Je clique. Je n'arrive pas.`
6. `Besoin : accéder au contenu`
7. `Préjudice : temps perdu, parcours interrompu`
8. `RÈGLES OPQUAST · CONSTRUIRE UNE RÈGLE`
9. `02 / 16`

Footer bottom left: `RÈGLES OPQUAST · CONSTRUIRE UNE RÈGLE`. Pagination bottom right: `02 / 16`.

Do not add any other text, number, error code, pseudo-text, logo, watermark, progress indicator, breadcrumb, photo, 3D rendering, texture, large gradient, check mark, data-loss symbol or certainty of abandonment. Generate one slide only.

## Critères d'acceptation

- Dimensions exactes 1672 × 941 px.
- Les neuf chaînes sont exactes.
- La relation lien interne vers contenu non atteint puis interruption est explicite.
- Aucune conséquence non sourcée n'est ajoutée.
